## 2. What data we collect

**a) Account data**
- Registered accounts: **email address**, **username**, and a **password** (transmitted over an encrypted connection and stored only in hashed/secured form on the server).
- Guest accounts: a **randomly generated user identifier** (and a generated secret); no email is required.

**b) Content you create ("user content")**
- Groups you create or join, group names and member lists, and group invitations.
- Expenses you add: amount(s), **currency/currencies (multiple currencies per expense are supported)**, description, date, who paid, and how the expense is split between members; and the balances derived from them.

**c) Device & technical data**
- A **push notification token** (provided by Firebase Cloud Messaging) together with the **device platform** (Android/iOS/Web) and your **app language/locale**, used to deliver notifications you have enabled.
- When your app communicates with our backend, standard request metadata such as your **IP address**, timestamps and request logs may be processed for operation and security.

**d) Stored only on your device (not transmitted to us)**
- App preferences (theme, in‑app language, notification toggle).
- Authentication tokens, stored encrypted using the platform secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).
- An offline cache of your groups and expenses, so the App works without a connection.

We do **not** use advertising, and we do **not** integrate analytics, crash‑reporting or tracking SDKs. **Firebase Analytics is disabled.** We do not sell your personal data.
