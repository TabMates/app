# TabMates — Account & Data Deletion

**Android App:** TabMates (`de.tabmates.androidapp`)

This page explains how to request deletion of your TabMates account and the data
associated with it.

## How to request deletion

1. Send an email to **1kylit08@addy.io**.
2. Use the subject line **"Delete my account"**.
3. Include the **email address** you registered with, so we can locate
   your account.
4. We confirm the request by replying to your registered email address.
5. Your account and data are deleted within **30 days** of a confirmed request.

## What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove:

- Account credentials: your email address, username, and password.
- Your push-notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
- Device information we hold: platform and app language.
- Groups and expenses you created that are not shared with other members.

## What data may be retained, and for how long

- Expenses and balances you shared inside a group are **retained or anonymized** so
  the remaining group members keep a consistent record of shared spending.
- Encrypted backups are purged within **90 days**.
- Where we are legally required to keep certain records, we retain them for the
  legally mandated period and delete them afterwards.

## Data stored only on your device

Uninstalling the TabMates app removes all locally cached data (groups, expenses,
and login tokens) from that device.
